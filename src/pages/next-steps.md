If you're on this page, you've probably just recieved your solder kit. Congrats!

### How do I solder?

What you need:

- your PCB 
- the parts you want to solder on it
- a soldering iron
- solder wire
- a well-ventilated space

You can also watch this [HOW TO SOLDER! (Beginner's Guide)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3jAw41LRBxU) YouTube video!

### After assembly
